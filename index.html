Munch

Snap it. Track it. Done.

Munch is a camera-first food and drink log. Take a photo of your meal (or type a quick description) and Munch estimates what's in it, including calories, macros and key nutrients, then keeps a simple daily record for you.

How it works

  • Snap a photo of a meal or drink, or describe it in a few words.
  • Refine: if something is unclear (a portion size, a brand, how it was cooked), Munch asks a short question. Answer it or skip it.
  • Review your day and your nutrient totals over time.
  • Set goals in your own words, up to three, so Munch highlights the nutrients that matter to you.
  • Read insights: short notes on what published research says about those nutrients, with links to the sources.
  • Save meals you eat often and log them again in one tap.

Nutrition estimates in Munch are approximate. Munch is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.

Munch is a subscription, monthly or yearly, with a 7-day free trial for eligible new subscribers. You buy and manage it through the App Store or Google Play. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own purposes, such as advertising.
